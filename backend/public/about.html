About Fendor

A warmer, more trustworthy way for Nigerians abroad to shop for home.

Fendor exists for a simple reason: sending gifts, groceries, and thoughtful support to loved ones in Nigeria should not feel uncertain. Too often, diaspora customers in the UK, USA, Canada, and beyond are left to figure out local vendors, payment trust, and delivery coordination on their own.

We are building a more confident path. Fendor bridges the gap between Nigerians abroad and verified local vendors, creating a curated marketplace experience backed by careful local coordination inside Nigeria.

How the model works

What stays manual in Phase 1 and why that matters.

We are intentionally keeping some operations manual in the MVP so the customer experience stays more careful, responsive, and trustworthy while the business takes shape.

What Fendor handles manually right now

+
Order confirmation

We manually confirm recipient details, availability, and delivery area before moving forward.

+
Payment review

Payment confirmation is reviewed manually instead of relying on automated checkout.

+
Vendor coordination

We follow up directly with local vendors to keep fulfillment aligned with the order.

Why this helps the MVP

For a trust-sensitive business like diaspora gifting, automation is not the first job. Credibility is. In Phase 1, manual coordination helps ensure products are confirmed, questions are answered, and deliveries are followed through carefully.

As Fendor grows, more of this can be automated. For now, the goal is a polished storefront paired with human support that customers can rely on.

Questions people may ask

A few helpful answers about the Fendor model.

Is Fendor a full ecommerce marketplace already?

Not yet. Phase 1 is a curated MVP with manual order confirmation, payment review, and local coordination.

Who is Fendor built for?

Nigerians abroad who want a more reliable way to send authentic gifts, groceries, and support back home.

Why does manual support matter?

Because trust is central. Manual support helps us confirm details carefully and reduce avoidable problems early.

Will more automation come later?

Yes. Future phases can expand into checkout, dashboards, inventory sync, tracking, and fuller backend automation.
