# Abuja delivery-rate research — provisional local testing

Research date: 5 November 2026. Origin assumption: central Kubwa; exact street, parcel weight, service type, rider availability and vendor pickup are unknown. This is not a contract, a verified quote, a market average or a complete list of the FCT. Do not publish rates as live prices until confirmed with local couriers.

## Route-specific leads

The search engine's cached excerpt for https://embeenexus.com/services.html lists Kubwa ↔ Wuse ₦4,500; Maitama ₦5,000; Jabi ₦4,000; Utako ₦4,000; Kado ₦3,500; Wuye ₦4,500; Life Camp ₦2,500; Lugbe ₦6,500. The page returned 404 when directly opened, so these are unverified single-provider cached figures, not averages. They are used solely as labelled local test data.

https://www.penglogistics.com/p/delivery-company-in-abuja.html advertises prices starting at ₦4,500 and quotes exact pickup/drop-off routes individually. https://services.travo.ng/courier-services/pickup-and-delivery-services-in-abuja-fast-reliable-logistics-by-travo-ng/ cites older cross-city ranges and notes that weight, distance and urgency affect prices. Neither is a second quote for the same Kubwa routes.

FCT has six area councils, not just central Abuja: https://www.fcta.gov.ng/faq/. A complete inventory of every settlement and door-to-door price cannot be derived from these sources. Areas without route-specific evidence are not assigned an invented rate. Obtain at least three comparable recent courier quotes per route for the same pickup address, destination, package size and service level, then calculate and approve the commercial rate.

The marketplace currently charges by destination; vendors may have different pickup locations. Kubwa-origin figures do not predict costs for other vendor origins or cover vendor-to-packaging pickup legs. Validate operating model and unit economics before enabling live rates. Existing locally configured test rates remain labelled as test data.
